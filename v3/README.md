# V3 eye-level desk layout
Preview: /v3/. One Three.js unit = one metre. Eye height is estimated at 1.62m for a person 5 ft 8 in tall. The original CC0 desk mesh is 1.80 × 0.657 × 0.549 m; its leg sections are extended to a 0.75 m working height without stretching the top assembly. Camera height stays fixed throughout the walk except slight head bob; mobile adapts distance and field of view. CRT and closed notebook use the imported GLBs, scaled uniformly to representative sizes. Other accessories remain rotated footprint guides until their assets are ready. Front direction of the CRT is assumed +Z and needs visual confirmation. All positions and yaws in arrangement array. No production changes.
