# V3 desk walk-up study
Open /v3/ on the preview deployment or run npx serve from repo root. The existing CC0 Poly Haven wooden desk is kept at 1.8m wide (uniform scale). Warm walnut-like tint, understated room wall, studio HDRI and static shadows. Mouse movement or touch drag accumulates walk progress, with damped approach, subtle head bob and a fixed arrival camera. W / Up / Space advance, Esc or Skip goes to the final view, Replay resets. Reduced-motion visitors skip immediately. No continuous GPU rendering while idle. Actual study desk replacement and accessories remain future steps.
